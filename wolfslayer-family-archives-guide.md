# Who's Who in the Wolfslayer Family Archives

This guide helps viewers identify people appearing in the Wolfslayer Family Archives. For surname history, see [Origins of the Wolfslayer Surname](/origins-of-the-wolfslayer-surname).

---

## People

**Audrey J. Walker** (1935–1976) — First wife of Kenneth. Mother of Daniel. Died before the archive period.

**Daniel G. Wolfslayer** (b. 1963) — Called "Dan." Son of Kenneth and Audrey. Married to Melissa. Father of Jordan and Ryan. Frequently behind the camera.

**Dorothy Churchfield Pavlik** (1929–2022) — Called "Grandma Dot." Mother of Melissa.

**Jordan Wolfslayer** (b. 1993) — Now Jordan Wolfslayer Hill. Daughter of Daniel and Melissa. Older sister of Ryan.

**Kenneth Wolfslayer** (b. 1934) — Son of Valdamir and Sigrid. Brother of Merna. Raised at the Balfour Drive house. First married to Audrey, later remarried. Father of Daniel.

**Melissa Pavlik Wolfslayer** (b. 1960) — Daughter of Dorothy. Married to Daniel. Mother of Jordan and Ryan.

**Merna Wolfslayer** (1936–2020) — Daughter of Sigrid and Valdamir. Sister of Kenneth. Never married. Lived at the Balfour Drive house her entire life—born in the living room, died there 84 years later.

**Ryan Wolfslayer** (b. 1989) — Son of Daniel and Melissa. Younger brother of Jordan.

**Sigrid B. Omholdt Wolfslayer** (1903–2003) — Called "Grandma Wolfslayer." Matriarch of the Wolfslayer family. Married to Valdamir. Mother of Kenneth and Merna. Lived at the Balfour Drive house with her daughter Merna.

**Valdamir H. Wolfslayer** (1897–1996) — Called "Val." Patriarch of the Wolfslayer family. Married to Sigrid. Father of Kenneth and Merna. Purchased the Balfour Drive house.

## Places

**Balfour Drive House** (4165 Balfour Drive, Detroit, Michigan) — Purchased by Valdamir, this house served as the Wolfslayer family home for nearly a century. At various times housed four generations, including Valdamir's parents Louis Wolfslayer (b. 1870) and Celia Engelman Wolfslayer (b. 1875). Kenneth was raised here. Merna was born in the living room and never left—she lived here her entire life, first with her parents, then with Sigrid after Valdamir's death, then alone until her own death in 2020. Sold shortly after.
