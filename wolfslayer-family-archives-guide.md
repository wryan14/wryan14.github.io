# Who's Who in the Wolfslayer Family Archives

This guide helps viewers identify people appearing in the Wolfslayer Family Archives. For surname history, see [Origins of the Wolfslayer Surname](/origins-of-the-wolfslayer-surname).

---

## People

**Audrey J. Walker** (1935–1976) — First wife of Kenneth. Mother of Daniel, John, Christopher, and Debra. Died before the archive period.

**Bret Calderwood** — Son of Rebecca and Robert Calderwood. Ryan's cousin.

**Brian Brady** — Husband of Cyndi Brady.

**Christopher Wolfslayer** — Called "Chris." Son of Kenneth and Audrey. Brother of Daniel, John, and Debra.

**Cyndi Brady** — Close childhood friend of Melissa Wolfslayer. Married to Brian Brady.

**Daniel G. Wolfslayer** (b. 1963) — Called "Dan." Son of Kenneth and Audrey. Married to Melissa. Father of Jordan and Ryan. Brother of John, Christopher, and Debra. Frequently behind the camera.

**Debra Wolfslayer** — Daughter of Kenneth and Audrey. Sister of Daniel, John, and Christopher.

**Dorothy Churchfield Pavlik** (1929–2022) — Called "Grandma Dot." Mother of Melissa and Rebecca. Sister of Hazel.

**Hazel** — Sister of Dorothy Pavlik. Ryan's great-aunt.

**John Wolfslayer** — Son of Kenneth and Audrey. Brother of Daniel, Christopher, and Debra.

**Jordan Wolfslayer** (b. 1993) — Now Jordan Wolfslayer Hill. Daughter of Daniel and Melissa. Older sister of Ryan.

**Kenneth Wolfslayer** (b. 1934) — Son of Valdamir and Sigrid. Brother of Merna. Raised at the Balfour Drive house. First married to Audrey, later to Ulla. All children are from his first marriage to Audrey. Father of Daniel, John, Christopher, and Debra.

**Meg Wolfslayer** — Former wife of Christopher Wolfslayer.

**Melissa Pavlik Wolfslayer** (b. 1960) — Daughter of Dorothy. Sister of Rebecca. Married to Daniel. Mother of Jordan and Ryan.

**Merna Wolfslayer** (1936–2020) — Daughter of Sigrid and Valdamir. Sister of Kenneth. Never married. Lived at the Balfour Drive house her entire life—born in the living room, died there 84 years later.

**Rebecca Calderwood** — Called "Becky." Daughter of Dorothy Pavlik. Sister of Melissa. Married to Robert Calderwood. Mother of Bret.

**Riggie** — Brother-in-law of Dorothy Pavlik. Ryan's great-uncle.

**Robert Calderwood** — Called "Bob." Husband of Rebecca. Father of Bret.

**Ryan Wolfslayer** (b. 1989) — Son of Daniel and Melissa. Younger brother of Jordan.

**Sigrid B. Omholdt Wolfslayer** (1903–2003) — Called "Grandma Wolfslayer." Matriarch of the Wolfslayer family. Married to Valdamir. Mother of Kenneth and Merna. Lived at the Balfour Drive house with her daughter Merna.

**Ulla Wolfslayer** — Second wife of Kenneth.

**Valdamir H. Wolfslayer** (1897–1996) — Called "Val." Patriarch of the Wolfslayer family. Married to Sigrid. Father of Kenneth and Merna. Purchased the Balfour Drive house.

## Places

**Balfour Drive House** (4165 Balfour Drive, Detroit, Michigan) — Purchased by Valdamir, this house served as the Wolfslayer family home for nearly a century. At various times housed four generations, including Valdamir's parents Louis Wolfslayer (b. 1870) and Celia Engelman Wolfslayer (b. 1875). Kenneth was raised here. Merna was born in the living room and never left—she lived here her entire life, first with her parents, then with Sigrid after Valdamir's death, then alone until her own death in 2020. Sold shortly after.
